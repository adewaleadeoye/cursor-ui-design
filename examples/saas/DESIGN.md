# Example — SaaS marketing + app

This is an **example**, not a brand.

## Surfaces

- Public landing: `marketing` — one hero, rail-aligned sections, dark-anchor CTA band.
- Authenticated app: `app` — sidebar or top nav; settings as stacked sections.
- Billing tables: `data`.

## Do not

- Purple gradient + glass cards + Inter as the identity.
- Hover-scale on every card.
- Marketing padding on settings pages.

## Color

Two-shade seed. Do not use `--primary` as the page headline color.
